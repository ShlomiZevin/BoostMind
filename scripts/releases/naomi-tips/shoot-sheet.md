# Shoot sheet: naomi-tips-1 ("4 ״אל תצפו״", Dr. Naomi, no speech)

Look: mock-naomi-A2.jpg (she sits in her armchair under her three diplomas).

## Setup
- **One take of about 60 s**, no talking. Upload it to the Drop folder **naomi-tips-1**.
- **Costume:** Naomi, same as the podcast: the wig, the glasses, the zebra blouse, dark trousers.
- **Seat:** sit on a chair or armchair in front of a plain wall, with good light on your face. The studio cuts you out and puts you in her clinic.
- **Phone:** vertical, at your seated eye level, about 80 cm away. Frame from the knees up, with your head about half-way up the frame. The top third of the frame gets covered by the text cards, so leave headroom.
- Look into the lens. Hands folded on your knee. Keep the movement small: she is an expert.

## Beats (the cards' timing in the project, 1×)
| Time | On screen | Naomi |
|---|---|---|
| 0–4.5 s | the title | Look at the camera, one slow serious nod. |
| 4.5–16.5 s | 1. שעות ההגעה | Calm, a small understanding "mm" nod, then adjust the glasses once. |
| 16.5–27.6 s | 2. סמכות ארגונית | Head tilted, a compassionate "the poor child" look. |
| 27.6–41 s | 3. ביקורת | Serious, then a short warm validating smile at the "וואו!" (around 35 s), then back to serious. |
| 41–52 s | 4. לעבוד | Completely serious. A slow, knowing nod, eyes closed for a beat, then straight into the lens. |
| 52–55 s | the end card (her book + logo) | Hold still. |

The timing is only a guide. The cards move to fit your take and the music.
