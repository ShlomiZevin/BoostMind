# naomi-1 - shoot sheet (ד"ר נעמי־ציפורה, a GUEST on someone else's podcast)

**Reader:** project `naomi-1` (the script is in it). **Upload:** shlomiverse.com/drop/ -> folder `naomi-1`.
This episode she is a **guest on someone else's podcast** - not her own show, not her clinic. The editor's project is ready: the host's studio (bright wood + plants, her seat in the centre, a mic on an arm from the right), her name band, the end card - the takes drop straight in.

## Look - match her 2025 reels so she is recognised
- Black bob wig, the same thin glasses, the blue-white zebra blouse, the same face filter as 2025.
- A mic in front of you is welcome (the set already has one reaching in from the right at chin height - if you use yours, bring it in from the right the same way, or leave it out).

## Framing
- Vertical, phone at eye height, ~60 cm away, you sitting. Head in the upper third; chest + mic in frame.
- Keep the hands inside the frame; the lower third gets her name band.
- Behind you: any plain, light, evenly lit wall - the podcast set is added in post. Light on the face from the front, no window behind.

## Where she looks
She is the guest, answering the HOST, who sits off-frame LEFT. Eyes to the host - a hand-width left of the lens the whole time, never into the lens (a clip from someone else's podcast, not a talk to camera). Body turned a little toward the left too.

## The host (off-screen woman, it's her podcast)
**We make her** (Danit, ElevenLabs) - you only film Naomi. It's already on the timeline.
Only the tail of her question is heard, for a moment, as if we joined mid-question: ״...בעצם דולה רגשית, נכון?״ - straight into "נכון."
Start the take with ~1 s of listening to the host (eyes left, a small nod, as if hearing the end of her question), then "נכון."

## Pace
Calm, slow, sure of herself, warm, zero winking. Leave the pauses - we cut every silence. One long take or a take per paragraph, both fine.
A stumble = say the whole sentence again (we keep the later one). Don't stop the recording in the middle of a sentence.

## Extra takes the editor wants (2-3 of each)
1. ״נכון. אז שמי דוקטור נעמי־ציפורה בן־נון־פרידמן־שטרנבך, ואני דולה רגשית.״ - the name in one breath, proud.
2. ״תינוק במונחים תעסוקתיים״ - dry, clinical, like a diagnosis.
3. ״את המשימה, אגב, הוא עדיין לא התחיל.״ - a beat before "אגב".
4. ״אבל הוא מרגיש את ה”נו” שלא כתבת.״ - let the "נו" land.
5. ״ואת יודעת מה קרה? אחרי שבועיים הבחור שלח לה מיוזמתו הודעה:״ - stop after "הודעה:" and hold 1 s (the socials version drops here).
6. ״איך מבקשים מהעובד להתחיל לעבוד.״ - then 2 s of silence, serious, still looking at the host (the end card cuts in on it).
7. 5 s of silent listening to the host (eyes left) - nodding, "mm" face (for the opening and cut-aways).

## The socials drop ("signal lost")
Right after ״אחרי שבועיים הבחור שלח לה מיוזמתו הודעה:״ - before ״“אני לא יודע איך להתחיל את המשימה.”״
