# אתה לא בורג - Osnat recruits Gen Z

## His words (2026-11-07)
- "lets plan a hr game for my character osnat in the shlomi verse"
- "lets do recruit gen z each one with his own ridiculous requests and expectations that osnat has to meet"
- Picked: Diner Dash on the office floor.
- "why missiles though? i had it in several videos just because it was war time here, now it's not" -> NO missiles,
  no war, no end of the world. A normal, bright Techy-Tech office.

## Decisions
- Mechanic: Diner Dash (proven). Character: Osnat, Head of People at Techy-Tech (profile: fake laugh, "ככה",
  recruiting buzzwords, the snack bag she "tries not to touch").
- World: Techy-Tech - a serious company. The humour is in the situation and in Osnat's lines, never in the company's
  own text. No real brands (the delivery bags in her videos = an invented brand, or plain bags).
- Candidates: Gen Z young people (several looks, boys and girls). True to the verse's Gen Z: they barely speak out
  loud - they sit right in front of her and TEXT their demands; their thoughts float over them ("רגע... היא מדברת
  אליי פיזית?"). The Gen Z cashier (genz) can show up as a special candidate.
- Phone first, upright. Hebrew, RTL.

## The loop (one day = one round, ~3 min)
1. A candidate walks into the lobby -> drag (or tap, then tap a desk) to seat them at a free interview desk.
2. Their phone pops 1-3 demands as text bubbles (icons + a short word).
3. Osnat runs to the perk stations along the bottom and brings what they want (carries up to 2 at a time).
4. All demands met -> the contract appears -> tap it -> "נקלט/ה!", Osnat's fake laugh, + points. The desk is free.
5. Each candidate has a patience bar (phone battery icon). It runs out -> they ghost (👻 "נעלם/ה"), a strike.
6. Points: per hire + speed bonus + combo for hires in a row (Diner Dash chaining = same station twice in a row).
7. Day goal: X hires. Each day = more candidates, more demands each, a new station unlocked.

## Osnat's tools
- **צחוק מזויף** (button, cooldown): everyone's patience stops for a few seconds. Her trait as a tool.
- **The snack bag** - a funny beat, not a tool: when 2+ candidates are about to ghost she takes a big bite
  ("אני משתדלת לא לגעת").
- Her lines in a strip above the play (her speech strip, never over the desks): "עברתי על הלינקדאין שלך ומאוד
  התרשמתי", "אתה לא בורג במערכת, אתה ככה... עמוד השדרה שלה", "זה לא 'בסדר', זה אתגר אמיתי", "קיבלת את המייל שלי?"

## Score / end
- Score = points for the day; daily record (VerseFrame.score, record unit "נקודות", better "high").
- Lose = 3 ghosts. Win the day = hit the hire goal -> next day. Endless days after day 5.
- End screen: hires, ghosts, best combo + "אסנת בשלומיוורס ←" (her wiki page).
