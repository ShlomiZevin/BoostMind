A blank pharmacy prescription label sticker, the kind an Israeli health-fund pharmacy prints on a thermal printer and sticks on a pill bottle. Portrait orientation (taller than wide). White glossy sticker paper with slightly rounded corners and a soft curl, a thin printed border, a narrow coloured header band at the top, a real-looking barcode printed along the bottom. The text area in the middle is EMPTY (no words anywhere) because the game prints the patient and medicine text on it. Game asset, front view, isolated on a transparent background.
