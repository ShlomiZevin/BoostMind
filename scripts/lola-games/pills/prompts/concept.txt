A screenshot of a finished, polished mobile puzzle game in portrait orientation (phone screen), painted illustration style, warm and cosy, high quality casual-game art.

The game: Lola, a young caregiver, has to fit an old grandmother's medicines into her weekly pill organizer. Every pill is a real medicine with its real look - small white round aspirin, yellow heart-shaped bisoprolol, peach round warfarin, purple-and-pink omeprazole capsules, big white oblong metformin, an amber fish-oil softgel, a huge chalky calcium tablet. Bigger pills take more room in the organizer, like puzzle pieces.

Top of the screen: a slim strip with the two characters as painted busts - on the right, Grandma (the first reference photo: curly grey hair, thin glasses with red sides, red lipstick, grey zip jacket) peering suspiciously over her glasses; on the left, Lola (the second reference photo: young woman, black bob haircut with a fringe, black zip track jacket) smiling sweetly. A small speech bubble from Grandma in Hebrew. A score.

Middle: the big weekly pill organizer seen from above on a wooden kitchen table, seven day columns with Hebrew day letters, some compartments already filled with pills.

Bottom: three pill pieces waiting to be dragged in, on the table.

Background: Grandma's living room, a colourful painting of birds on the wall behind, softly blurred.
