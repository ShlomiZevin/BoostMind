The cover picture of a Hebrew comedy mobile game, "Millimeters" (מילימטרים): the player is the physiotherapist of Yaniv, an information-security engineer whose whole body hurts - no doctor ever finds anything, two years of physiotherapy only made it worse, and his range of motion is "millimeters". The cover is the game's card on the games page and its key art.
