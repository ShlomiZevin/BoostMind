A concept picture of a finished mobile game screen (vertical phone, upright) for a Hebrew comedy game.
The game: Yaniv, an information-security engineer whose whole body hurts (no doctor ever finds anything), is at his physiotherapy session. The player is the physiotherapist: with one finger the player slowly moves one of his limbs through a stretch, but his range of motion is "millimeters" - only a tiny green zone along the movement path counts, and going a hair too far makes him scream in pain.
References: the first picture is Yaniv's portrait, the second is how he looks in his videos. Keep his face exactly - bald head, thin metal glasses, short stubble, his eyes and nose - painted as a warm, funny, high-quality illustrated game character (not a photo), wearing a grey tank top. He lies or sits on a physiotherapy treatment bed in a small clinic, his face exaggeratedly suffering and dead serious about it.
On screen: the arm being stretched with a thin curved movement path and a very small glowing green zone on it, the player's finger on the path, a pain meter at the top, a small "session" counter, and a speech strip at the bottom with one Hebrew line: "מילימטרים! אמרתי לך מילימטרים!". Big readable game UI, made for a phone.
