A painted game sprite for a Hebrew comedy mobile game, "Millimeters": the player is the physiotherapist of Yaniv (the man in the reference - same skin, same arm hair, same painting style, warm and funny, high quality), whose whole body hurts. In the game, this body part lies on the treatment bed and the player slowly bends its joint with one finger.
What the game needs from the picture: the body part alone, straight, lying horizontally across the picture, with the joint that bends near the middle of the picture, covered by a medical support that is clearly visible. Nothing else in the picture, transparent background, the whole body part inside the frame. The game will cut the picture at the support and turn the far side, so it must be one continuous, straight piece.
The body part:
his right lower leg and bare foot seen from the side, from just below the knee on the left to the toes on the right, foot relaxed, a black ankle brace on the ankle.
