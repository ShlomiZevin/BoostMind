## The game
- Top-down night park, painted. He walks (thumb joystick / swipe); off his own ground he leaves a glowing trail; back on
  his ground the loop closes and the area becomes his (dim purple). Score = % of the park + conversations.
- **Strangers stroll the paths, each with a clue = which story he tells**: dog on a leash (the dog trainer story),
  protein shaker (creatine), phone (reels), laptop / headphones (AI). When he comes near one, he starts the small talk:
  his innocent question in the speech strip ("סליחה, אפשר לשאול אותך משהו?").
- **The small-talk clock**: each line he says goes one step darker (question -> details -> hints -> the reveal), and
  her face follows (polite -> confused -> horrified). **Close the loop around her before he reaches the reveal** = she is
  "trapped in the conversation": the story plays to its end, big points, she flees the park in horror. Too slow = the
  reveal comes out early, she screams, runs and **calls the police** (one more police car).
  -> the player's choice every time: a big loop (more ground, slower) or a tight one (safe, small).
- **Police cars** patrol the paths; touching his open trail cuts it = he loses a life (3). His own body touching his
  trail also cuts it (Paper.io rule).
- **The restraining-order circle** (red dashed, around "her" house at the edge of the park): stepping in = sirens,
  every police car comes for him.
- Nights = levels: each night more strangers, more police, a new story clue unlocked. Daily record = the night's score
  (VerseFrame.score), same park for everyone each day (seeded).
- His lines are only his real ones from the scripts (+ new ones in the same voice, short). The dark part stays words
  only: nobody is shown tied or hurt; the girls just run.

## Tutorial
who you are -> the goal (take the park) -> how you lose (police cut your trail, your own trail, the red circle) ->
walk + leave a trail -> close a loop (try it) -> a stranger: her clue = his story -> close around her before the
reveal (try it) -> police -> the red circle -> ready?
