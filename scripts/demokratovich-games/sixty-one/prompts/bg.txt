The background painting for an upright phone game "61 - רוב קואליציוני" (a Stack tower game: a coalition tower of party slabs is built in the middle of the screen in 3D and grows up, the camera slowly rises with it from the floor to the ceiling).
The place: the inside of a small, old, slightly shabby community hall called "בית המשפט שערי בג״ץ" - a community court-synagogue of a rabbi whose faith is democracy and the rule of law. A wooden ark-like cabinet with golden scales of justice instead of any religious symbol, old wooden benches, a worn carpet, framed old documents and a ballot box on a shelf, warm lamps. High walls going up to a tall painted ceiling with a skylight, so the picture works from floor (bottom) to ceiling (top).
The middle of the picture stays empty and calm (the tower stands there) and the lower right corner stays open floor (a figure will stand there). No people, no text except the small plaque. No crosses, no religious symbols, no Torah scrolls, no Hebrew letters on the ark.
Same warm 3D toy-film look as a stylized animated movie.
