A flat, straight-on (orthographic, no perspective) texture of the UPPER part of a tall interior wall of an old warm community court-synagogue hall, to be placed above a smaller room's wall in a 3D game - same style and colours as the reference room (cream plaster with soft cracks, warm carved wood trims, brass). From bottom to top: a carved wooden cornice band at the very bottom, then a tall plastered wall with three very tall narrow arched windows letting in golden afternoon light, framed old documents between them, and at the very top a deep carved wooden ceiling cornice with a painted pale blue sky ceiling edge. Fills the whole picture edge to edge, no floor, no furniture, no people, no text, no religious symbols.
