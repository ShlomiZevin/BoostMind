## Decisions
- Mechanic: Stack (tap to drop a sliding slab; the overhang is cut off; a perfect drop keeps the width). Not used in any verse/Plaxzy game yet.
- Story: every slab is a party with seats. You are building a coalition: 61 = a government ("והלכתא: קמה ממשלה"), then on toward 120 ("כנסת שלמה"). The score = seats; the daily record = seats.
- Real 3D: three.js; the tower in 3D; the rabbi = the rigged figure from verse-planet (characters/demokratovich/web/demokratovich-main.glb, compressed) on a podium, reacting with his clips/gestures: beard_stroke (thinking), lilmodcha (a perfect drop), rachmana (a big cut / collapse), cheer (61), make_a_call (the Rabbanit).
- Invented party names only (no real parties): e.g. "בית הימין", "בית השמאל", "גוש השינוי", "הגוש השני", "רשימת הפשרה", "תנועת הרוב המיוחס"...
- Twists (small, keep it simple):
  - 3 perfect drops in a row = "הרכב מורחב": the slab grows back a little.
  - The Rabbanit calls with a poll: the phone rings, he answers ("רגע אחד, זו הרבנית"), the next slab slides faster for a few drops; he hangs up "לא מכניסים הביתה סקרים".
- Lines: his halachic/Talmudic voice, from the profile catchphrases ("רחמנא ליצלן", "ללמדך ש...", "והלכתא", "מיתיבי... לא קשיא") - his line strip at the bottom, never over the play.
- Sounds: ElevenLabs - slab thuds (variants), a cut slice, perfect chime, phone ring, gavel at 61, a calm niggun-free court/synagogue-hall music loop.
- Home: public/demokratovich-games/sixty-one/, draft until he says release.

## Tutorial steps
1. You are the rabbi's coalition builder (גבאי הקואליציה).
2. Goal: stack seats to 61 - then as high as you can, up to 120.
3. How you lose: a slab that misses the tower completely.
4. Tap = drop the slab (try it).
5. What hangs over is cut off - the tower narrows.
6. A perfect drop keeps the width; 3 in a row = הרכב מורחב.
7. The Rabbanit's poll: the slab speeds up for a while.
8. HUD: seats counter + bar to 61, best of the day.
