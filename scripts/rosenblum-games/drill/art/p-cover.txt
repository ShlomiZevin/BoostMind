The cover picture of a mobile game about a shelter drill in an Israeli high school (tichon), painted in the cartoon style of the first reference picture. At the centre, Teacher Rosenblum (the man in the second reference picture: late 50s, messy grey wig with bangs, thin metal glasses, short beard, black polo shirt) stands on a school stairwell landing, one hand raised giving orders, peering strictly over his glasses. Around and behind him a long, orderly column of 16-17 year old high-school students (teen proportions, white school t-shirts, jeans, backpacks) walk down the stairs two by two holding hands; one lanky boy walks alone at the very end of the column; one student is sneaking toward a toilets door, another holds a football. A red siren light glows on the wall. Wide picture, the teacher's face clear in the middle, empty-ish space at the top for a title, no text.
