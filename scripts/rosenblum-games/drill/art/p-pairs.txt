A sprite sheet for a 2D side-view game: Israeli HIGH SCHOOL students (teenagers, 15-17 years old, tichon - NOT little kids) walking two by two, holding hands, during a school shelter drill. They wear plain white school t-shirts, jeans, sneakers, backpacks.
IMPORTANT: they are 16-17 year old high-school students (Israeli tichon, grade 11) - tall, teen proportions with longer legs and bodies and smaller heads than children, teenage faces, some boys with a little stubble or messy hair, girls with teen hairstyles; NOT children, not middle school.
Grid of 4 columns x 3 rows. Each ROW is a different pair of teens (row 1: a boy and a girl; row 2: two boys; row 3: two girls), each pair always the same two people with the same clothes across its row. The 4 columns are the 4 frames of a smooth walking cycle (legs and arms swinging), the pair holding hands in every frame.
Same painted cartoon game style as the first reference picture (the game's concept screen). Transparent background. Side view, every figure facing RIGHT, full body head to shoes, nothing cut off, generous empty space around each figure, figures not touching each other, all figures the same size and standing on the same foot line in their row.
