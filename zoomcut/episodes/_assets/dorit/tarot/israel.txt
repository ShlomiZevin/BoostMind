A single tarot card in the classic Rider-Waite style: hand-drawn black ink outlines, flat muted watercolour fills (mustard-yellow sky, ochre, brick red, cornflower blue), a simple ornate border, a small blank cartouche at the top and a blank title banner at the bottom (both left empty). The card stands straight and fills most of the frame, on a plain flat light-mint (#CDEBDD) background around it. No text, no letters, no numbers anywhere. A mighty bull (Taurus) bucking wildly on rocky ground under a stormy sky, a blue-and-white banner tied to its horns flapping, the Taurus constellation glowing above, everything shaking and unstable.
