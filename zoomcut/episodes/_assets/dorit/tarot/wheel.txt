A single tarot card in the classic Rider-Waite style: hand-drawn black ink outlines, flat muted watercolour fills (mustard-yellow sky, ochre, brick red, cornflower blue), a simple ornate border, a small blank cartouche at the top and a blank title banner at the bottom (both left empty). The card stands straight and fills most of the frame, on a plain flat light-mint (#CDEBDD) background around it. No text, no letters, no numbers anywhere. Card 'Wheel of Fortune': a great wheel floating in the clouds with blue ballot boxes fixed all around its rim, spinning round and round, a tired sphinx sitting on top, little ballot papers flying off.
