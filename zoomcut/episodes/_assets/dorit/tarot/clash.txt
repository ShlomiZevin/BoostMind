A single tarot card in the classic Rider-Waite style: hand-drawn black ink outlines, flat muted watercolour fills (mustard-yellow sky, ochre, brick red, cornflower blue), a simple ornate border, a small blank cartouche at the top and a blank title banner at the bottom (both left empty). The card stands straight and fills most of the frame, on a plain flat light-mint (#CDEBDD) background around it. No text, no letters, no numbers anywhere. Card 'The Chariot' gone wrong: an empty stone chariot being pulled in opposite directions by a bull and a mountain goat, their horns locked, sparks flying; in the sky above, a pale-blue ringed planet (Uranus) frowning down at them.
