A funny candid photo in a small, very messy Haredi family apartment in Bnei Brak: piles of laundry on the sofa, a stroller, shoes and toys everywhere, a dining table full of plates and cups, supermarket bags on the floor, a bookcase of holy books, and through the window old apartment buildings with laundry on the balconies. On the floor sits the little boy from the left of the reference (same face, black velvet kippah, curly side-locks, white shirt, tzitzit, navy trousers, socks), holding his socked foot with a big comic "ouch!" face, mouth open, eyes squeezed shut - he just stepped on a toy brick. Around him many small golden plastic building bricks (a cheap LEGO-like knock-off) from the toy set in the middle of the reference, a half-built golden altar model and the open blue toy box. Right next to him a chubby baby of about one year in a onesie sits on the floor and stares at him, puzzled, wondering what happened - the baby clearly looks like the bearded man on the right of the reference (the same round face, warm brown eyes, chestnut-auburn hair), but of course without a beard. Warm daylight, photographed at child height, wide angle. Realistic. No text.
