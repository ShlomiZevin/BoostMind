A majestic, dignified painting of a great old tree planted by streams of water, as in Psalm 1:3: a wide ancient oak with deep strong roots reaching into a clear flowing stream that runs across the foreground, a thick textured trunk, a broad full crown of rich green leaves heavy with golden fruit. Warm Jerusalem-stone and parchment tones, soft golden late-afternoon light, a classic oil-painting style of a Torah-family heirloom print, calm and respectful. The upper quarter of the frame is a quiet pale parchment-coloured sky with no branches, kept empty for a title. No people, no animals, no letters, no text, no frames.
