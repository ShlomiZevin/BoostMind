Fast, funny, cheeky comedy caper, 126 BPM, in the style of a sneaky pizzicato caper. Plucky pizzicato strings and a nagging, nasal bassoon that keeps butting in with the same pushy little motif like a friend who never stops talking, a honking tuba on the downbeats, bongos and finger snaps, a kazoo and a squeaky clarinet answering him back, mischievous xylophone runs. The sound of the annoying friend everyone is sick of - he shows up uninvited, sits on your couch and won't leave. Busy and bouncy from the first second, no slow intro, no breakdown, keeps building. Instrumental, no vocals.
