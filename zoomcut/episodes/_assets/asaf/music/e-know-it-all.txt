Fast, playful comedy caper, 132 BPM, sneaky and bouncy. Staccato pizzicato strings, a smug bassoon and a whiny oboe arguing over a walking upright bass, an oom-pah tuba, bongos and handclaps, little xylophone and glockenspiel runs that climb like a worry he keeps inflating, sly muted trumpet stabs. Mock-suspense for the know-it-all pessimist friend who turns every tiny thing into a disaster and everyone rolls their eyes at him. Driving and full from the first bar, no slow intro, no ambient parts, no breakdown. Instrumental, no vocals.
