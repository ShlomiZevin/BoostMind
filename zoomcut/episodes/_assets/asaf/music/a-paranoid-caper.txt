Fast, funny, bouncy comedy caper, 128 BPM. Sneaky pizzicato strings and a cheeky bassoon trading quick staccato licks over a walking upright bass, bongos and finger snaps, a tuba punching the downbeats, little xylophone runs that rise like a worry getting bigger. Playful mock-suspense, the feeling of a gossipy friend making a mountain out of a molehill. Driving and busy from the first second, no slow intro, no breakdown, energy keeps building. Instrumental, no vocals.
