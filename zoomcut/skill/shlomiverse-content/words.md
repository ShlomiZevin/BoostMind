# The Shlomiverse's words - one vocabulary for every line a visitor reads

His rule (2026-11-17): "a strict and coherent methodology, line and phrasing". Every line a visitor reads - the app, the
pages, the emails, the chat, ציפציפ, a story, a Terra brief for copy - uses these words. The mythology itself (what the
world is, the founders' legend) is on the guide's page: Backstage → דמויות → המדריך והמיתולוגיה (the chat reads it from there).

## What the Shlomiverse is
A real world - a planet of its own, a parallel universe. The people in it are real and live their own lives; the videos
are moments from their lives. Nobody made them up, wrote them, plays them or was inspired by someone for them.

**Every video is a live broadcast** (his framing, 2026-11-18): something happening right now in the verse, broadcast
from there. The socials catch the broadcast only until the reception drops (the "signal lost" ending - the edit-video
skill, rule 9); inside the Shlomiverse the broadcast goes on. Words for it: שידור, קליטה, הקשר עם יקום שלומיוורס, "השידור
ממשיך בשלומיוורס", "הקליטה חזרה" - never נעול, פרימיום, המשך יבוא, a paywall's words. No LIVE / "שידור חי" tag in the
app for now (his call).

## The words
| Say | Never |

| סרטון (a moment from someone's life); a group (משפחת פרידמן, טקי־טק) = עולם / עולמות | פרק, מערכון, סדרה, סדרות, פרודיה, סאטירה; production talk (כתיבה, צילומים, תסריט) |

| a request / message / idea goes to השלומיוורס or to המייסדים | לשלומי, "תגיע לשלומי", any real name |
| השלומיוורס | "של שלומי", "שלומי והדמויות", שחקן, מגלם |
