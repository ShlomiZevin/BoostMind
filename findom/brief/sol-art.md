## 1. Name + tagline

Keep **Sable Court**, subject to trademark/domain clearance; it has authority without sounding explicit.  
Replace the tagline with **“Devotion, duly entered.”**  
“Choose your queen” feels like a dating game; “enter her world” is generic fragrance copy. The alternative owns the court-and-ledger language.

## 2. Design direction critique

The black–bone–oxblood palette, fashion serif, brass rules, wax seals and “old money” cues are luxury-brand shorthand. Combined with crowns, cards and leaderboards, this could become costume aristocracy layered over a standard creator marketplace.

1. **Build the homepage as a court docket:** asymmetric 12-column composition, oversized featured dossier, narrow register and persistent ledger—not a centered hero followed by floating cards.
2. **Turn queen cards into case files:** square corners, strict portrait crops, vertical nameplates, accession numbers and a fixed terms strip. No generic badges or pill-shaped tags.
3. **Create a proprietary “Terms Slip”:** every paid CTA pairs price, unlock, AI status and current spending cap in one flat, ruled component. Make this the signature conversion device.
4. **Own the typography:** replace DM Serif Display/Cormorant duplication with one commissioned SC ligature and a small custom titling alphabet; reserve mono strictly for prices, limits and records.
5. **Use oxblood overprints as interface states:** ENTERED, LIMIT SET, PAUSED, RITUAL COMPLETE crossing rules and images. Replace green presence dots with textual states such as PROTOCOL ACTIVE.
