## 4. Chat UX: the six that matter most

1. **Queen Presence Strip**  
   Avatar, current mood, availability, and persistent “AI Character” label. Her status should feel alive: “Reviewing reports,” “Not accepting excuses,” “In a generous mood.”

2. **Fixed Tribute Drawer**  
   One-tap, clearly priced tribute actions. Always show the exact amount and what changes after purchase: attention, ritual unlock, task, priority response.

3. **Ritual + Task Cards**  
   Structured actions with accept, decline, complete, and timer states. This turns chat into a game-like relationship rather than a blank messaging window.

4. **Allegiance Status**  
   “Visitor” → “Initiated” → “In Her Court” → “Inner Circle.” Use “Owned” only as an opt-in label chosen by the user, never as a default status.

5. **Attendance Streak**  
   A non-monetary return mechanic: consecutive ritual check-ins, not consecutive purchases. Allow users to hide it.

6. **Personal Ledger + Limits**  
   Current session total, weekly total, pre-set cap, pause button, and a clear “You are at 80% of your limit” notice. This is both safer and more premium than pretending limits do not exist.

## 5. Design direction

### Mood
**Private members’ salon meets editorial fashion house meets old-money ledger.**  
Think lacquered stationery, severe tailoring, gallery placards, hotel bar menus, wax seals, black-and-ivory print—not neon nightlife, cam-site UI, purple smoke, or SaaS dashboards.

### Palette
- **Court Black** — `#171716`
- **Bone Paper** — `#F0EBDD`
- **Oxblood** — `#6E2028`
- **Tarnished Brass** — `#A7834E`
- **Slate Ink** — `#59605C`
- **Signal Red** — `#B6322D`
- **Soft Stone** — `#C9C2B5`

Use black, bone, and oxblood as the core. Brass is an accent, not a metallic gradient.

### Typography
- **Headlines:** `DM Serif Display`  
- **Body/UI:** `Manrope`  
- **Rules, prices, timestamps, ledger details:** `IBM Plex Mono`  
- **Optional character quotes / editorial pull lines:** `Cormorant Garamond`

### Texture and material cues
- Uncoated bone paper backgrounds
- Black lacquer panels
- Fine engraved-rule borders
- Letterpress-style labels
- Hairline brass dividers
- Editorial image crops with visible grain
- File-tab navigation and ledger-table layouts
- Red stamp motif for “Entered,” “Approved,” “Ritual Complete”

No gradients. No glows. No glassmorphism. No floating purple blobs.

### Logo concept
A restrained **SC monogram** constructed like a legal seal or archival ex libris mark—interlocking serif letters inside a thin square frame. Pair it with a spaced uppercase wordmark:

**SABLE COURT**

The mark should feel stamped onto a black invitation or debossed into cream stationery, not like an app icon.
