THE BOOK

The next stage of human evolution is technical support.

“What happens when creation itself no longer needs us?”

We have already learned how an unremarkable ape conquered the planet, and we have imagined that ape becoming a god. In Homo Useless, we arrive at the next, more modest stage of our story: standing next to the router, asking it to please work.

Drawing on history, biology and forty-seven terms-of-service agreements nobody read, Yuval Know All Harari asks the question Silicon Valley would rather you didn't: when the algorithms can write, drive, diagnose and compose better than we can, what exactly are we for?

The answer, it turns out, is checking whether there is an Internet connection. And, as the final chapter warns, even that job is not safe.
