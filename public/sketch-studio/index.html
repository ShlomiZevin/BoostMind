Sketch Studio

A personal video studio by Shlomi Zevin

Sketch Studio is the private editing tool Shlomi Zevin uses to make his own short comedy videos. When a video is finished, the studio can upload it to his own accounts on Instagram, TikTok, YouTube and X, so he doesn't have to move files to his phone first.

What it does

  • Edits his own footage into vertical short videos.
  • Uploads a finished video to the accounts he has connected: an Instagram Reel, a TikTok draft (which he finishes and posts in the TikTok app), a YouTube Short, or a post on X.
  • Posts only when he presses Publish, and only to his own accounts.

Who uses it

Only its creator. Sketch Studio is not offered to other people, has no sign-up, and doesn't access anyone else's account.
