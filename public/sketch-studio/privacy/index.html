Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

1. Who this covers

Sketch Studio ("the app") is a personal video tool made and used by Shlomi Zevin. It works only with his own social accounts. It doesn't collect information from anyone else.

2. What the app receives

When an account is connected (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube or X), the platform gives the app:

  • an access key that lets it upload videos to that account;
  • basic account details such as the account name and ID, used only to show which account is connected.

The app does not read messages, followers, contacts or any other account data.

3. How it is used and kept

  • The keys are stored only on the creator's own computer, in a private file that isn't shared or published.
  • They are used only to upload videos when he presses Publish.
  • Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • To hand a video to a platform, the app may place it in private cloud storage for up to an hour, behind a private time-limited link. It is deleted after the upload.

4. Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from the platform's own settings (for example, TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions). When access is removed, the stored key stops working.

5. Children

The app isn't directed at children and isn't used by them.

6. Changes

This policy may be updated. The date at the top shows the latest version.
